About SOIR

Soir means evening

The name is French for evening, and that is what we are built around: the hour when the day slows down and the table fills up.

We sit on VIP Road in Temri, beside The Lush, so you can find us easily after work or on a weekend drive. Our Instagram, @soir.bythelush, is where we share new looks and updates.

  • A contemporary, atmospheric space with a distinct identity
  • Food and drinks under one roof
  • Easy to find on VIP Road, Temri
